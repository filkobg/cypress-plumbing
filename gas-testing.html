Common Questions About Gas Testing & Compliance

How often do daycares and care facilities require gas testing?

In our area, licensed daycare centers generally require certified gas safety inspections every two years. Assisted living, retirement, and foster homes typically mandate strict annual safety sign-offs to maintain operational licensing.

Do you handle the city permits and utility coordination?

What happens if a gas pressure test fails?
