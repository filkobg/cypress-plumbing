💧 Ceiling Drips & Staining

Brown water rings or active dripping coming through your drywall usually points to a compromised supply line or an issue from an upstairs bathroom.

🪵 Swollen Walls & Warping Floors

If the base of your drywall is buckling, soft to the touch, or your wood/laminate flooring is changing color and buckling, water is pooling underneath.

📈 Unexplained Water Bill Spikes

If your monthly utility bill suddenly shoots up for no obvious reason, you likely have a silent continuous leak running behind your walls or under your slab.

Common Questions About Residential Leaks

Should I shut off my main water valve if I notice a ceiling drip?

Yes. If you see active dripping coming through a ceiling, locating and shutting off your home’s main water supply valve is the fastest way to stop additional water from feeding the leak and causing more structural damage while you wait for a plumber.

How do you track down a leak behind a wall or under the floor?

Instead of relying on guesswork or tearing up random drywall, we use systematic diagnostic logic—isolating fixture lines, checking pressure drops, and evaluating where the moisture is traveling to pinpoint the exact failure point before making a cut.

Can an unexplained high water bill really mean a hidden leak?

Absolutely. If your usage habits haven't changed but your utility bill climbs significantly, it's one of the clearest indicators of a silent leak (such as a continuous underground supply line issue or a running toilet valve) wasting water around the clock.
