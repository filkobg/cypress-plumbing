Common Questions About Sub-Slab & Sewer Line Replacements

How do you handle under-slab foundation drain pipe failures?

We map out the exact path of the failure, evaluate options for tunneling or minimal-disruption excavation, and replace compromised sub-slab sections with heavy-duty, code-compliant piping designed to maintain correct drainage grade.

What indicates a broken sewer line between the house and the city main?

Frequent sewage backups across multiple fixtures, slow-draining tubs, wet spots in the yard, or foul odors around the property typically indicate a structural failure, root intrusion, or collapse in the main line leading to the city connection.
